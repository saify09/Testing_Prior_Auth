1. Executive Summary 
1.1 Purpose 
This Product Requirements Document (PRD) defines the comprehensive requirements for 
an AI-driven Prior Authorization (PA) system designed to streamline healthcare 
authorization processes while maintaining HIPAA compliance and human oversight at 
critical decision points. 
  
2. Product Overview 
2.1 Product Vision 
To create the most efficient, accurate, and compliant prior authorization platform in 
healthcare, leveraging AI to eliminate administrative burden while ensuring clinical 
appropriateness and patient safety through intelligent human oversight.

2.3 Key Features Overview 
• Intelligent Document Processing: OCR, NLP extraction, auto-classification of PA 
requests 
• AI Clinical Criteria Engine: Automated matching against MCG/InterQual guidelines 
• Risk-Based Routing: Smart triage to auto-approve or human review queues 
• Clinical Review Workbench: Unified interface for RN/MD review with AI 
recommendations 
• Real-Time Status Portal: Provider and member self-service tracking 
• HIPAA Compliance Framework: Built-in encryption, audit logging, access controls 
• Appeals Management: Automated tracking, deadline management, outcomes analysis 
• Analytics & Reporting: Dashboards for performance, quality, and compliance metrics 
  
3. Business Requirements 
3.1 Business Objectives 
• BR-001: Reduce PA processing costs by 60% within 12 months of launch 
• BR-002: Achieve minimum 70% auto-approval rate for routine medical requests 
• BR-003: Decrease average turnaround time to <24 hours for 80% of requests 
• BR-004: Improve provider satisfaction scores from 42% to 85%+ 
• BR-005: Reduce appeals rate from 15-20% to <8% through improved accuracy 
• BR-006: Generate positive ROI within 18 months of deployment 
• BR-007: Scale to process 1M+ PAs annually without linear cost increase 
3.2 Regulatory & Compliance Requirements 
• BR-101: Achieve and maintain HIPAA compliance for all PHI processing 
• BR-102: Meet state-specific PA turnaround time requirements (varies by state) 
• BR-103: Comply with CMS prior authorization requirements for Medicare Advantage 
• BR-104: Support NCQA accreditation standards for UM programs 
• BR-105: Enable audit trail for regulatory examinations (DOI, CMS, etc.) 
• BR-106: Maintain Business Associate Agreements with all vendors 
• BR-107: Support member appeal rights per state and federal regulations 
  
4. Functional Requirements 
4.1 Request Intake & Validation 
• FR-001: Accept PA requests via multiple channels: EHR integration (HL7/FHIR), secure 
portal, fax, EDI 278 
• FR-002: Perform OCR on faxed/scanned documents with 98%+ accuracy for typed text 
• FR-003: Extract key data elements: patient demographics, diagnosis codes, procedure 
codes, clinical notes 
• FR-004: Validate member eligibility in real-time against payer systems 
• FR-005: Check for duplicate/existing PAs for same patient/service 
• FR-006: Auto-request missing information with templated outreach to providers 
• FR-007: Generate unique PA tracking number upon successful intake 
4.2 AI Clinical Criteria Matching 
• FR-101: Match diagnosis and procedure codes against evidence-based medical 
necessity criteria (MCG, InterQual) 
• FR-102: Analyze submitted clinical documentation for supporting evidence 
• FR-103: Check medication requests against formulary and step therapy requirements 
• FR-104: Identify missing clinical information required for determination 
• FR-105: Generate confidence score (0-100%) for approval/denial recommendation 
• FR-106: Provide explainable rationale for AI recommendation with supporting citations 
• FR-107: Flag edge cases and ambiguous scenarios for human review 
4.3 Human-in-the-Loop Review 
• FR-201: Route cases to appropriate review queue based on complexity and risk score 
• FR-202: Present AI recommendation with full supporting evidence to clinical reviewer 
• FR-203: Enable reviewer to accept, modify, or override AI recommendation 
• FR-204: Require physician review for all proposed denials before finalization 
• FR-205: Support peer-to-peer consultation requests from treating providers 
• FR-206: Allow reviewers to annotate cases with clinical rationale 
• FR-207: Track reviewer agreement/disagreement rates with AI for quality monitoring 
4.4 Decision Communication 
• FR-301: Generate determination letters with clinical rationale and appeal rights 
• FR-302: Deliver notifications via provider portal, secure email, and postal mail 
• FR-303: Update EHR systems with authorization details via HL7/FHIR 
• FR-304: Send member notifications in plain language with next steps 
• FR-305: Provide alternative treatment options for denials when applicable 
• FR-306: Include appeal instructions and deadlines in all denial communications 
• FR-307: Support multi-language notifications (English, Spanish minimum) 
4.5 Appeals Management 
• FR-401: Accept appeals via portal, fax, mail with automated acknowledgment 
• FR-402: Track regulatory deadlines (30-day standard, 72-hour expedited) 
• FR-403: Route to independent reviewer (different from initial determination) 
• FR-404: Provide access to complete original case file for appeal review 
• FR-405: Support external review process when internal appeals exhausted 
• FR-406: Analyze appeal outcomes to identify systemic issues and AI improvement 
opportunities 
• FR-407: Generate appeal overturn reports for quality monitoring 
  
5. Technical Requirements 
5.1 System Architecture 
• TR-001: Microservices-based architecture for independent scaling and deployment 
• TR-002: Cloud-native deployment (AWS/Azure/GCP) with multi-region availability 
• TR-003: API-first design with RESTful and GraphQL endpoints 
• TR-004: Event-driven architecture for real-time processing and notifications 
• TR-005: Containerized deployment (Docker/Kubernetes) for portability 
• TR-006: Service mesh for secure inter-service communication 
• TR-007: Separate data plane for PHI processing with enhanced security controls 
5.2 AI/ML Platform 
• TR-101: NLP models for clinical document understanding (transformer-based) 
• TR-102: Classification models for medical necessity determination 
• TR-103: OCR engine with handwriting recognition capabilities 
• TR-104: Named entity recognition for extracting ICD-10, CPT, NDC codes 
• TR-105: Confidence scoring algorithm with calibrated probabilities 
• TR-106: Model versioning and A/B testing framework 
• TR-107: Continuous learning pipeline with human feedback incorporation 
• TR-108: Explainable AI (XAI) framework for transparency and auditability 
• TR-109: Bias detection and fairness monitoring across demographics 
5.3 Data Management 
• TR-201: HIPAA-compliant data lake for all PA-related data 
• TR-202: Separate databases for PHI vs. de-identified analytics 
• TR-203: Data retention policies aligned with state/federal requirements (7-10 years) 
• TR-204: Automated data backup with point-in-time recovery 
• TR-205: Data encryption at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3) 
• TR-206: Data anonymization/de-identification for AI training datasets 
• TR-207: Master data management for providers, facilities, and payers 
5.4 Integration Requirements 
• TR-301: HL7 v2.x interface for legacy EHR systems 
• TR-302: FHIR R4 API for modern healthcare interoperability 
• TR-303: X12 EDI 278 support for payer-to-payer communication 
• TR-304: Secure FTP/SFTP for batch file exchange 
• TR-305: Single Sign-On (SSO) via SAML 2.0 and OAuth 2.0 
• TR-306: Webhooks for real-time event notifications 
• TR-307: ETL pipelines for claims system integration

7. User Experience & Interface Design 
7.1 Design Principles 
• Simplicity: Minimize cognitive load with clean, intuitive interfaces 
• Efficiency: Reduce clicks and keystrokes for frequent tasks 
• Transparency: Make AI recommendations explainable and visible 
• Consistency: Use standard patterns and terminology throughout 
• Accessibility: Ensure usability for all abilities and devices 
• Trust: Build confidence through clear communication and reliability 
7.2 Key User Workflows 
Provider Portal - Submit PA Request 
1. 1. Login with SSO credentials 
2. 2. Search for patient by name/ID or scan insurance card 
3. 3. Select service/procedure requiring authorization 
4. 4. Upload supporting clinical documentation (drag-and-drop) 
5. 5. Review auto-populated form with extracted data 
6. 6. Submit request and receive confirmation with tracking number 
7. 7. View estimated decision timeframe based on case complexity 
Clinical Reviewer Workbench - Review Case 
8. 1. Login to secure workbench with MFA 
9. 2. View prioritized queue sorted by urgency and deadline 
10. 3. Click on case to see AI recommendation with confidence score 
11. 4. Review patient history, diagnosis, clinical notes in unified view 
12. 5. Expand AI rationale section to see supporting evidence and citations 
13. 6. Click "View Guidelines" to see applicable medical necessity criteria 
14. 7. Make determination: Approve / Deny / Pend for more info 
15. 8. For denials: physician counter-signature required before finalization 
16. 9. Add clinical notes (auto-saved every 30 seconds) 
17. 10. Click "Finalize Decision" to generate determination letter 
Member Portal - Check Status 
18. 1. Login with member ID and DOB or create account 
19. 2. View dashboard with all active and recent PA requests 
20. 3. Click on specific PA to see detailed status timeline 
21. 4. View determination letter and appeal instructions if applicable 
22. 5. Initiate appeal directly from portal if desired 
23. 6. Download PDF copies of all communications 
24. 7. Set up text/email notifications for status updates

Dashboard - Clinical Reviewer

• Center Panel (55%): Case queue with filters (urgency, deadline, type), sortable

indicators 
• Right Panel (25%): AI insights summary, recent activity feed, compliance alerts, 
performance metrics 
Case Detail View - Clinical Reviewer 
• Header Section: Patient demographics, insurance info, urgency badge, days remaining 
until deadline 
• Tab Navigation: Clinical Summary | Documentation | AI Analysis | Guidelines | History 
• Clinical Summary Tab: Diagnosis codes with descriptions, requested 
procedure/medication, relevant vitals and labs, timeline of treatment 
• AI Analysis Tab: Confidence score (large visual gauge), recommendation 
(approve/deny), supporting rationale with expandable evidence, conflicting factors if 
any, similar cases comparison 
• Action Panel (Bottom): Decision buttons (Approve/Deny/Pend), clinical notes text 
area, peer-to-peer request button, save draft button

8.2 Data Privacy Requirements 
• PR-001: De-identification of PHI for all analytics and AI training datasets 
• PR-002: Data masking in non-production environments (no real PHI in dev/test) 
• PR-003: Patient consent tracking for data usage beyond 
treatment/payment/operations 
• PR-004: Right to access: members can download their PA history within 30 days 
• PR-005: Right to rectification: process for correcting inaccurate data 
• PR-006: Data portability in machine-readable format (JSON/CSV) 
• PR-007: Privacy impact assessments for new features processing PHI 
8.3 AI Governance & Ethics 
• AG-001: Model transparency: document training data sources, features, and limitations 
• AG-002: Bias testing across race, age, gender, and geographic demographics 
• AG-003: Regular fairness audits with statistical parity analysis 
• AG-004: Human oversight required for all high-risk determinations (denials, high-cost 
services) 
• AG-005: Explainability: AI must provide clinical rationale for all recommendations 
• AG-006: Model drift monitoring with alerts for performance degradation 
• AG-007: Version control for all AI models with rollback capability 
• AG-008: Third-party validation of AI accuracy at least annually 
• AG-009: Clear escalation path for AI errors or unexpected behavior 
• AG-010: AI decision appeals process separate from standard PA appeals

11.5 Go-Live Strategy 
• Pilot Phase: Launch with 1-2 provider groups and limited PA types, monitor closely 
• Phased Rollout: Gradually expand to additional providers, geographies, and service 
types

• Support Model: Enhanced support during first 90 days with dedicated hotline 
• Communication Plan: Regular updates to stakeholders, providers, and members
